Report a problem

Include your device and browser or Android version, the steps you took, what you expected, and what happened. A problem URL and the approximate time can help us investigate.

Please do not send passwords, reset links, session tokens, hosting API keys, or payment details.

Password recovery

Choose Forgot your password? on the sign-in screen and enter your account email. Check your inbox and spam folder for the reset email. The link expires after 30 minutes and can be used once. If it expires, request a new link.

A successful reset signs out earlier sessions and removes their notification device registrations. Sign in again on each device and check that Daily reminders is enabled to resume Android reminders.

Account and privacy requests

For account deletion, contact us from the email used for your AnkiDSA account. Deleting the browser extension does not delete your server account. You can delete individual problems and their notes through the app.

Current beta

Problem capture, notes, synchronization and spaced reviews are available. Android reminders are available in the notification-enabled APK: open the account menu, choose Reminders, turn on Daily reminders and allow Android notifications. Password recovery is available from the sign-in screen.
